On Saturday, I attended a Schönefeld community event that I can summarize as a commemoration of the past, a celebration of the present, and a presentation and consultation of the future.

## Past 🛬

Berlin Airport Terminal 5, also known as the former DDR airport building, ceased operations in 2022 due to low passenger demand. With this event, the community had the opportunity to say goodbye to Terminal 5 with a tour of the building. In the future it is going to become the Quartier SXF 2.0.

## Present 🏙

Because of the 20th anniversary of Schönefeld City, there were activities for all ages: food, music, and games for children and adults. The city's mayor was there sharing with the attendees. The location was ideal, as "the airport and the community are intimately linked".

## Future 🤝

One of the things that caught my attention the most was the presentation of the future plans for the areas adjacent to the terminal and the idea of being more than just an airport city. There was a citizen consultation activity where attendees could express what they would like for the future by mapping desired places and future investments in the area.

It was an inspiring experience for urban planners.
