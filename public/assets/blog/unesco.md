I'm really happy to have been part of enhancing the **UNESCO Sites Navigator** at Mapular. 🙂

Working with large-scale spatial datasets, leveraging Esri's powerful GIS tools once again 🩷, and knowing this work contributes to protecting heritage sites worldwide as part of UNESCO's mission made this project especially meaningful. 🌍

## What the solution includes

- A web app integrating 40+ environmental & risk layers
- Automated spatial risk analyses with alert system
- An interactive dashboard for monitoring all UNESCO-designated sites

## Current alert types

- Vegetation disturbance
- Coral stress
- Earthquakes
- Tsunamis
- Fires

## Which alerts are showing up today?

- ⚠️ Vegetation disturbance detected in Canaima National Park (my home country 🇻🇪)
- ⚠️ Coral stress detected near the Lagoons of New Caledonia

Seeing global monitoring in action and even identifying risks in places that are personally meaningful truly shows the power of geospatial intelligence to protect our world heritage. 🌍

A true pleasure to work with UNESCO on this initiative.
